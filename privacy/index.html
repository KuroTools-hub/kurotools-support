本アプリのPDF処理はユーザーの端末上で実行されます。KuroToolsは、PDF本文、ファイル名、処理履歴、端末識別子を収集またはKuroToolsのサーバーへ送信しません。

3. 端末内に保存する設定

表示言語など、アプリの利用に必要な設定を端末内に保存する場合があります。これらはKuroToolsへ送信されません。

Privacy Policy

Effective and last updated: September 3, 2026

Scope

This policy applies to PDF.Flatten.Optimizer for Windows, provided by KuroTools.

PDFs and personal data

PDF processing is performed locally on the user's device. KuroTools does not collect or transmit PDF content, filenames, processing history, or device identifiers to KuroTools servers.

Local settings

The app may store settings required for operation, such as the display language, on the user's device. These settings are not sent to KuroTools.

Microsoft Store

Purchases, payments, licenses, and trials are handled by Microsoft Store and are subject to Microsoft's privacy policy.

Support

Information voluntarily submitted through GitHub Issues may be public. Do not submit confidential PDFs, personal information, or customer data. GitHub's privacy statement applies to information handled by GitHub.

Changes and contact

This policy may be updated when laws, features, or delivery methods change. The revised text and date will appear here. Please use the support page to contact KuroTools.
